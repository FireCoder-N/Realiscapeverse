# Realiscapevesre

## About
At [Realiscape](https://realiscape.ch/) we create next generation simulator systems for defense, security, industry and maritime.
Supplementary to our role as engineers, we also remain nerds at heart.

This repository acts as a "lair", where the entirety of our homebrew D&D (2024) campaign shall be archived.

## Website
The documentation of our campaign is available under [firecoder-n.github.io/Realiscapeverse](https://firecoder-n.github.io/Realiscapeverse/)

## Contribute
*A word for my fellow colleagues and co-players...*

... who are willing to help me expand the notes of our campaign.

1. First of all, clone the repository. — It is not Apex-sized, I promise.
2. If you own or would like to use Obsidian, then the homonymous branch is the right place for you. — Branch out of it and let the lore be documented.
   Otherwise, the `main` branch is the right place for you. There you will find a `public` folder, containing the already processed and rendered markdown files.
3. After you are satisfied with the documentation process, create a pull request for merging into the `main` branch. Changes shall and will then be reviewed by me and merged.
4. **Important!** the `gh-pages` branch is automatically generated by npm and is the actual place where our wiki resides. — It is a bit shy though, so don't meddle with it and let it be.
5. 🪄
